Accountability
Online features are account-gated because if a player breaks the rules — cheating, harassment, or unsportsmanlike conduct — we need to know who they are in order to issue a ban or time-out. Anonymous players cannot be held responsible for their actions.

Rewards & Tournaments
Tournaments award prizes and recognition to winners. Without an account, there is no way to verify who won, track standings, or deliver rewards to the right person. Your account is your identity in competition.
